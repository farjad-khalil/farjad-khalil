I build and ship full-stack products end to end, from system design and data modeling through to production-ready interfaces.

My work centers on business-critical platforms: client management systems, administrative dashboards, payment and inventory workflows, and the APIs and integrations that hold them together. I care about software that stays fast, maintainable, and easy to extend long after the first release.

---

## 🧭 How I Work

- **Architecture first.** I design systems around clear boundaries, predictable data flow, and schemas that hold up as requirements change.
- **Ownership across the stack.** I take features from database to deployment, with no hand-offs required.
- **Built for maintainability.** Typed codebases, modular services, and patterns the next engineer can follow.
- **Security and access by design.** Authentication and role-based access are part of the foundation, not an afterthought.
- **Pragmatic AI and automation.** I integrate AI where it removes real friction from a product or workflow.
